    Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so the saves you keep matter more than the order you play in. Work the five stages in order; the long counters fill in as you travel.

      The 100% checklist (A Long Road)

      Open it in game at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story. It needs, in full: all 8 bank vaults and all 39 gang operations, the underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold medal, all drone races and Bret’s 15 Target Decks. The rest is a share: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. Nothing on it is missable.

      Save discipline

      • Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave).
      • Keep one untouched save from just before the final mission.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save — they switch off trophies and saving.
      • Turn on progress and collectible notifications in Settings so counters stay visible.

      Missable & time-sensitive trophies

      Built from the official descriptions and the launch-week guides. Only one trophy can actually be missed; one more has a time-sensitive step.

      What cannot be missed

      Every story trophy, every side-story finale (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and every free-roam trophy stays available after the credits. The 100% checklist has nothing missable on it. If you keep a save per chapter, nothing in this guide can cost you a second playthrough.

      Sources & verification

      Every trophy name, description and grade in this guide was checked on 19 November 2026 against the PS5 list as reported by Exophase, and cross-checked on GTABase, Output Lag and Press Start — all four agree on all 55. The guides are written in this guide’s own words; a source is cited, never copied. A link below opens only if you follow it — this app fetches nothing.

        What is not yet known

        Five trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. This guide says so on each of them instead of inventing a method. Fair Game’s exact triggering animal was also unconfirmed. Methods for the other 50 trophies come from the two launch-week guides above.
